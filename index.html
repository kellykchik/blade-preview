THE BASICS, EXPLAINED

A new search landscape.
A few good questions.

What is the difference between AEO and GEO?

AEO focuses on content that helps answer engines respond to questions. GEO focuses on how brands and content appear in generative AI responses. The two overlap, so Gladeanswers brings them into one view.

Which AI and search platforms are planned?

The proposed testing scope includes DeepSeek, Gemini, Google AI Overviews, and iOS Search. These are development targets rather than currently supported integrations. Coverage will be confirmed as the product is built.

How are citations different from recommendations?

A citation uses your website as a source. A recommendation suggests your business or product as an option. A brand can be cited without being recommended, so Gladeanswers’s planned analysis treats them separately.

Does Gladeanswers optimize a browser?

Browsers are the tools people use to access the web. Gladeanswers focuses on LLM responses, AI-generated answers, and the website content that supports them.

Can visibility in AI answers be guaranteed?

No. Responses vary by model, question, time, and context. Consistent questions and repeated observations can help you understand patterns and prioritize improvements.

Is this a live analytics dashboard?

This website currently presents a product prototype. The dashboard uses illustrative data. Live tracking, accounts, and connected data will be added as the product is developed.
