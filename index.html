THE BASICS, EXPLAINED

A new search landscape.
A few good questions.

What is the difference between SEO, AEO, and GEO?

SEO focuses on visibility in search engines. AEO focuses on content that helps answer engines respond to questions. GEO focuses on how brands and content appear in generative AI responses. The areas overlap, so Blade brings them into one view.

Does Blade optimize a browser?

Browsers are the tools people use to access the web. Blade focuses on the search results, LLM responses, and AI-generated answers people encounter, and the website content that supports them.

Can visibility in AI answers be guaranteed?

No. Responses vary by model, question, time, and context. Consistent questions and repeated observations can help you understand patterns and prioritize improvements.

Is this a live analytics dashboard?

This website currently presents a product prototype. The dashboard uses illustrative data. Live tracking, accounts, and connected data will be added as the product is developed.
